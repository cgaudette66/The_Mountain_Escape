Warm & Inviting

The Mountain Escape is a boutique outdoor retreat offering luxury canvas tent accommodations in a stunning mountain setting. Designed for rest and renewal, we invite guests to unplug from technology, reconnect with nature, enjoy incredible hiking, and experience the comfort of premium bedding beneath the stars. It's a place to slow down, breathe deeply, and leave feeling refreshed.

Coffee Barista Station
This Artisanal Manual Pour-Over Coffee station is an all-in-one setup designed for specialty coffee lovers who enjoy a mindful, hands-on brewing ritual. Blending vintage elegance with modern precision, this kit is perfectly suited for scenic outdoor camping.
Group Hikes
Discover the beauty of the mountains with our guided group hikes, designed for guests of all experience levels. Led by knowledgeable local guides, each hike takes you through scenic forests, peaceful meadows, and breathtaking overlooks while sharing fascinating insights about the area's wildlife, plants, and natural history. Whether you're seeking a leisurely nature walk or a more adventurous trek, our hikes provide the perfect opportunity to explore, connect with fellow guests, and experience the serenity of the outdoors.
Sunset Yoga & Meditation
As the sun dips behind the mountains, guests gather for peaceful outdoor yoga and guided meditation overlooking the valley. These sessions are designed for every experience level and offer the perfect way to unwind after a day of hiking and exploration.
